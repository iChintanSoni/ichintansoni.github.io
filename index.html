01 / About

Thirteen years of shipping, now pointed at agentic AI.

I'm an AI engineer and engineering leader based in Bengaluru. My career runs from native Android through full-stack web to cloud-native systems, and today it is focused on production agentic AI at IBM.

I build multi-agent systems with LangChain, LangGraph and retrieval-augmented generation that turn multi-day enterprise workflows into minutes, deployed on Kubernetes with full CI/CD ownership. Along the way I've led engineering teams of 20+, mentored 10+ engineers into senior roles, and kept writing, speaking and publishing open source.

How I work
